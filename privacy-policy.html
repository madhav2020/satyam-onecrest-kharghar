Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 November 2026

1. Who we are

This website is run by Reco Property LLP, an authorised channel partner for One Crest, a project by Satyam Developers (R. Gulati Group) and Metro Group in Sector 20, Kharghar, Navi Mumbai (MahaRERA PR1270002601519). It is not the developer's official website. This policy explains what details we collect on this website, why, and what you can do about it.

2. What we collect

  • Details you give us in a form: name, mobile number, email, the home type you're interested in, and what you asked for (price list, brochure, video or floor plan).
  • Visit details: pages you visit, device and browser type, the website that sent you here, and campaign tags in the link (for example, which ad you clicked).
  • A visitor ID: a random number that links your visit to your enquiry. It is not your name or number.

3. Why we use it

  • To reply to your enquiry and send what you asked for (price list, brochure, videos, floor plans).
  • To contact you about One Crest by phone call, SMS, WhatsApp or email, and to arrange site visits.
  • To see which ads and pages work, so we can improve them.
  • To follow the law, including MahaRERA rules.

4. Details kept in your own browser for 2 days

When you fill a form, your name, mobile number and email are saved in your own browser (not on any other device) for 2 days. This means you don't have to fill the form again for the price list, brochure, videos or floor plans. After 2 days they are deleted automatically. You can delete them earlier by clearing your browser's site data.

5. Cookies, analytics and ad tools

We use Google Tag Manager and Google Analytics to understand how the website is used, and advertising tools from Google and Meta to measure our ads. These tools use cookies and receive visit and event data (for example, that a page was viewed or a form was sent). You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings.

Showing our ads again (remarketing): Google and Meta may use cookies to show our ads to people who visited this website, when they later use other websites and apps.

Matching enquiries with our ads: When you send a form, we may send a scrambled (hashed) copy of your email address and mobile number to Google and Meta. They use it only to match it with their own ad records and to measure which ads brought enquiries. Google and Meta cannot read your original email address or mobile number from it. We do not send your name, address or what you asked for. You can ask us to stop this using the contact details at the end of this policy.

To control the ads you see, or turn them off:

  • Google: My Ad Center
  • How Google uses information from sites that use its services
  • Meta (Facebook and Instagram): Ad preferences

6. Who we share it with

We do not sell your details. We share them only:

  • with the developer's sales team for One Crest, to handle your enquiry;
  • with companies that run our systems for us (for example, our CRM hosting), only for that work;
  • with Google and Meta, as our analytics, advertising and ad measurement tools: visit and event data, and the scrambled (hashed) email address and mobile number explained in section 5;
  • when the law, a court or a government authority requires it.

7. How long we keep it

We keep enquiry details as long as needed to help you with your property enquiry, and longer only where the law requires it. You can ask us to delete them at any time.

8. Keeping it safe

We take reasonable steps to protect your details from misuse, loss and unauthorised access. No website or system is completely secure, so we can't promise absolute security.

9. Your rights

Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you can:

  • ask what details we hold about you;
  • ask us to correct or delete them;
  • withdraw your consent, including to calls and messages about One Crest;
  • raise a complaint with us, and if it isn't resolved, with the Data Protection Board of India.

To use any of these rights, contact us using the details below.

10. Children

This website is meant for adults. We do not knowingly collect details of anyone under 18.

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version.
