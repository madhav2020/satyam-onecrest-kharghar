## 3. About One Crest

**H2:** What is One Crest Kharghar?

One Crest Kharghar is a G+31 residential tower in Sector 20, Kharghar, Navi Mumbai, built by Satyam Developers and Metro Group. It was earlier marketed as Codename Showstopper. The project is registered with MahaRERA under number PR1270002601519.

One Crest offers 2, 3 and 4 BHK homes and Jodi units, with RERA carpet areas from 859 sq ft to 2,012 sq ft. Prices start at ₹2.48 Cr* for a 2 BHK Label home.

The developer calls One Crest "Navi Mumbai's first co-branded residences". The Stellar Lobby and the Verve Clubhouse are furnished with Roberto Cavalli Home Interiors, the home décor line of the Italian fashion house. The developer also says the homes have up to 30% larger carpet areas than comparable homes.

SEO: "first co-branded" and "30% larger" are written as the developer's claims, not as our own.

## 5. What the price includes

**H2:** What does the One Crest price include?

**Included in the agreement value:**
- Development charges
- Car parking: 1 for 2 BHK and 3 BHK homes, 2 for 4 BHK homes
- Clubhouse charges

**Paid extra:**
- Stamp duty: 7% of the agreement value (as per the developer's cost sheet)
- Registration: ₹30,000
- GST: 5% (paid with each payment stage)
- Legal charges ₹30,000 + GST, at registration
- Society formation ₹2,50,000 + GST, at possession
- 24 months' maintenance + GST, at possession
- 1% TDS, paid by the buyer to the government
- Property tax
