What is One Crest Kharghar?

One Crest Kharghar (also called Satyam One Crest) is a G+31 residential tower in Sector 20, Kharghar, Navi Mumbai, built by Satyam Developers and Metro Group. It was earlier marketed as Codename Showstopper. The project is registered with MahaRERA under number PR1270002601519.

One Crest offers 2, 3 and 4 BHK homes and Jodi units, with RERA carpet areas from 859 sq ft to 2,012 sq ft. Prices start at ₹2.48 Cr* for a 2 BHK Label home.

The developer calls One Crest "Navi Mumbai's first co-branded residences" and uses the tagline "The new crest of luxury". The Stellar Lobby and the Verve Clubhouse are furnished with Roberto Cavalli Home Interiors, the home décor line of the Italian fashion house. The developer also claims "30% larger carpet size" for these homes.
